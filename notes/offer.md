# Resume Studio — Offer set + warm-sell (DRAFT for approval)

Draft v1. Edits welcome. Prices are launch placeholders; the real anchor is the
**< $500** job-seeker pain, not features.

## Tiers (USD baseline; quote AUD locally)

| Tier | Includes | Price | Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rewrite** | Rewritten resume (targeted to one role), 2 revision rounds, plain-text + PDF layout note | $60 | Lead generator, low friction |
| **Rewrite + LinkedIn** | Rewrite + headline (3 options) + About section | $100 | Most popular |
| **Hunt bundle** | Everything above + 3 tailored cover letters (or 1 per application) | $160 | High AV; use for warm referrals |

- **Launch offer:** first 3 clients at 25% off in exchange for a written testimonial + permission to use in marketing. (Their name/reveal optional; you stay behind an alias for the gray work but this account is clean.)
- Delivery: 48h, 2 revision rounds included; extra rounds $20/tier.
- Money-back if they don't like the rewrite direction after round 1.

## Warm-sell script (network-first)

**Step 1 — Open (cold-ish DM/email to warm list).** No pitch, lead with a specific observation:
> "You mentioned hunting for a senior role — your resume is currently burying your `p95 latency` win on page 2. Want me to send over a rewritten summary section? Free, 5 minutes."

**Step 2 — Free taste.** Deliver just the summary/headline rewrite. Make it visibly better. Then:
> "That's the top third. The full rewrite + LinkedIn would be $100, 48h turn. Want it?"

Don't document the client's resume without permission; note consent + a talking-points file in the job answers.

**Step 3 — Close + follow-up cadence.** 2 follow-ups max (72h apart). No hard close, leave the tier + bundle in the reply so they can self-select. Lost after no reply.

## On ship
- Send deliverable + a 3-line "what I changed and why" note (shows care, gets testimonials).
- Ask: "If you're happy, one line about how it helped — a testimonial."
- Record the fee on the `/job/{id}` page (Mark shipped + income). First dollar shows on the dashboard.

## Guardrails (from the plan)
- Clean zone: no undisclosed AI. If asked, say drafts are AI-assisted and human-reviewed — true and fine.
- Don't fabricate facts; every claim in output must trace to the intake resume/answers.
- First 3 clients subsidized for social proof; after that, full price and raise as testimonials land.
